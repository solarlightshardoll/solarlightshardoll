## About Hardoll

Hardoll Enterprises LLP is an Indian solar lighting company working across residential, commercial and outdoor lighting applications.

The company's solar-lighting range includes solar street lights, flood lights, garden lights, pillar lights, wall lights, pathway lights, motion-sensor lights, road studs, UFO lights and other outdoor lighting solutions.

Founded in 2016, Hardoll is based in Kerala, India.
